About Zohara

Where creativity meets light.

Zohara Lighting is built around a simple idea: a thoughtfully designed lamp can change the feeling of an entire space.

Our collection brings together distinctive forms, warm illumination, and considered details. Each design is created to feel at home in your everyday surroundings while adding a memorable touch.

From a quiet reading corner to the center of a room, we want our lighting to make ordinary moments feel a little more special.
